**What students do**

Write the conditional logic: when to pit, how rain changes handling, what a puddle costs, and what adapting well is worth. Then run the same track dry and wet, with a pit stop and without.

**What is pre-built for them**

The weather generator, pit markers, puddle hazards, and the HUD.

**STEM skills applied**

Conditional branching, event handling, timing windows, and designing for conditions that change mid-run.

**Career lens**

strategist — Morgan.

**Planned size**

9 coded steps, plus an opening card and a closing card.

**What students should leave thinking**

*"The right call depends on conditions, and conditions move. I had to decide before I knew."*
